## Checks for primary ISO analysis

1. Confirm the exact byte length (IRC report: 136,398,848) and compute whole-image cryptographic hashes for the acquired image; compare provenance separately.
2. Confirm ISO 9660 volume identifier, bootability/catalog, sector count and precise directory entries/casing; reconcile the later README tree against raw directory records.
3. Inspect the boot catalog/configuration and distinguish the visible live-boot sequence from the scripts that implement it. Confirm the prime sequence, timing, pauses, message screen, reboot behavior, and Ctrl+C handling.
4. Inventory root filesystem paths, file sizes, modes, timestamps, hashes, ownership, and symlinks; test descriptions of `folly`, `wisdom`, all three data blobs, and the signed message against raw bytes.
5. Independently inspect 761.mp3 tags and stream properties; compute exact file size/duration and hashes. Distinguish metadata from claims about reversed instruments, hidden speech, and musical significance.
6. Verify the documented Twitter/560.13/761.mp3 XOR chain against the precise original inputs and recorded offsets; community summaries simplify this relation.

## Follow-up: detailed wiki text, XOR paths, and the `folly`/`wisdom` caveat

### The factorization is `1031 × 1229 × 1259`

The primary local wiki export’s 2013 article identifies the parable number as 1,595,277,641. Its companion 2014-page article writes the product as `1259*1031*1229`; a user-subpage transcription writes `1031 1229 1259`. Direct arithmetic confirms `1031 × 1229 × 1259 = 1,595,277,641`; substituting 1226 does not. The asserted 2013 telnet-prime gap is “between 71-1229,” and the explicit missing-prime list begins at 73 and ends at 1223, so 1229 is the excluded upper boundary. There is a real local transcription error: [archives/archive3/2013.md:152](https://github.com/cicada-solvers/documenting-cicada3301-scream314/blob/53e4282d89229b98212ef4f2349310fab6c43532/2013.md) prints `1259*1031*1226 = 1,595,277,641`; a wiki observations subpage repeats that error at [archives/uncovering-cicada-wiki-20260810/export/uncovering-cicada-wiki-export.xml:21732](https://uncovering-cicada.fandom.com/wiki/Instar_emergence_%28mp3_and_hidden_poem%29?oldid=44259). The local records are internally mixed: archive3’s third-line equation labels its sum 1229 but the listed addends using 363 total 1216; a wiki observations page uses 376 and 1229 in its line breakdown but then prints 1226 in the product. Other wiki equations give `1259*1031*1229`; multiplication confirms 1229 is correct. A second typo appears in [archives/archive3/2013.md:149](https://github.com/cicada-solvers/documenting-cicada3301-scream314/blob/53e4282d89229b98212ef4f2349310fab6c43532/2013.md): its third-line sum uses 363, which totals 1216, while the associated answer claims 1229. The main wiki’s line breakdown uses 376 for “divinity” and totals 1229.

Sources: [archives/uncovering-cicada-wiki-20260810/export/uncovering-cicada-wiki-export.xml](https://uncovering-cicada.fandom.com/wiki/Special:Export) (2013 article near lines 36595 and 36722; 2014 article’s Parable section near line 38186); [archives/uncovering-cicada-wiki-users-20260810/uncovering-cicada-wiki-users-export.xml](https://uncovering-cicada.fandom.com/wiki/Special:Export) (user transcription near line 7011); contemporary arithmetic discussion in [archives/archive2/2013/additional docs/logs/all cosmos posts from 7th on the 761 song - full log.txt:1823-1873](https://github.com/cicada-solvers/The-Complete-Cicada3301-Archive/blob/c3127e0e9eb5afbb582d37988d6369fe38429238/2013/additional%20docs/logs/all%20cosmos%20posts%20from%207th%20on%20the%20761%20song%20-%20full%20log.txt#L1823-L1873) and `:4001-4163`.

### Wiki XOR accounts compress distinct operations

The full main-namespace wiki article makes three separate claims that should not be collapsed into “761.mp3 XOR something = image”:

1. Reconstructing the tweeted binary feed and XORing it with `761.mp3` yields the final JPEG rune table. (Article near lines 36610-36612.)
2. The early/shortcut path used the partial tweet-derived JPEG and `560.13`: XORing those reportedly yielded a truncated Base64 payload plus repeated `33010` pattern; completing the pattern then XORing with `560.13` recovered the full JPEG. The article labels whether that shortcut was intended unknown. (Article near lines 36612-36625; the corresponding user-page copy appears near lines 6799-6830.)
3. A later telnet `hint`/`clue` response XORed with `_560.00` reportedly yielded “You can't see the forest when you're looking at the trees.” The article discusses this separately from the Twitter/Gematria chain. (Article near lines 36708-36722.)

The later analysis README in [archives/neuroretransmit-cicada/2013/003-twitter/README.md:40-70](https://github.com/cicada-solvers/neuroretransmit-cicada/blob/b13600373d3400d75ec4208198019f3d92a304dd/2013/003-twitter/README.md) describes the Twitter decoding as an `560.13 XOR 761.mp3 XOR twitter.bin` chain for an ASCII/Base64 intermediate and related reconstruction steps. The January 7 contemporaneous logs independently say “mp3 xor twitter xor data\560.13 gives base64 text” ([archives/archive2/2013/additional docs/logs/all cosmos posts from 7th on the 761 song - full log.txt:3197, 3288](https://github.com/cicada-solvers/The-Complete-Cicada3301-Archive/blob/c3127e0e9eb5afbb582d37988d6369fe38429238/2013/additional%20docs/logs/all%20cosmos%20posts%20from%207th%20on%20the%20761%20song%20-%20full%20log.txt)). Thus wiki shorthand (“761.mp3 XOR Twitter yields JPEG”) describes the final visible pairing but omits the `560.13` shortcut/intermediate path. `_560.00` belongs to the distinct telnet hint claim; do not substitute it for `560.13` in the Twitter operation.

A contemporaneous January 11 IRC correction explicitly says “wisdom and folly came from the CD, not from xor with .17” and “I don't think anything's been xored with .17 yet” ([reference-docs/irc/cyber-meme-tips-cicada-3209987udsj0x-20260810/2013/2013-Jan_-3301hackers.txt:1672-1673](https://cyber.meme.tips/archives/irc/cicada-3209987udsj0x/)). This cautions against explanations that treat the `folly` and `wisdom` rootfs files as results of XORing `560.17`.

### `folly` and `wisdom`: identical, usage not settled

The later README says the two `/tmp` files have exactly the same contents and “no known use” ([archives/neuroretransmit-cicada/2013/002-3301/README.md:159-167](https://github.com/cicada-solvers/neuroretransmit-cicada/blob/b13600373d3400d75ec4208198019f3d92a304dd/2013/002-3301/README.md)). Wiki article text says they are exactly the same and “Neither appear to represent a file type” (main export near line 36725). The archive3 summary similarly says their use is unknown ([archives/archive3/2013.md](https://github.com/cicada-solvers/documenting-cicada3301-scream314/blob/53e4282d89229b98212ef4f2349310fab6c43532/2013.md), system section near lines 160-165).

There is, however, a counterclaim in the Uncovering Cicada user-page export: a user reports remembering “these files being part of a successful XOR attempt in 2014,” explicitly tagged `[evidence needed]`, and another comment says “there was a non public data output derived from wisdom and folly ... lurker and soulseekah were able to get something,” without a reproducer or artifact. The same page includes an unsubstantiated philosophical explanation linking the filenames to a 2012 discussion. Treat all of those as recollections/hypotheses, not a resolved use. In the January 2013 IRC extraction, participants say the files are identical (Jan. 6 #CicadaWiki historical copy at [archives/uncovering-cicada-wiki-20260810/export/uncovering-cicada-wiki-export.xml](https://uncovering-cicada.fandom.com/wiki/Special:Export) within “Files found in Cicada OS”) and later note `/tmp` contents. The January 11 correction only resolves that the pair came from the CD and was not produced by XOR with `.17`; it does not prove they were never used in some later, separate 2014 attempt.

The local evidence therefore supports the wording “identical files; purpose/use unverified,” not categorical “never used” or a definite XOR role. A useful follow-up, if the parent ISO-analysis lane allows it, is to hash and byte-compare both raw rootfs files, then search the 2014 logs/artifact trail for the recollected output and its exact inputs. No raw files were accessed during this documentary-only follow-up.

## Follow-up: LSS16 splash decode

The extracted [output/iso-audit/iso-files/BOOT/ISOLINUX/SPLASH.RLE](iso-files/BOOT/ISOLINUX/SPLASH.RLE) was decoded by the bounded, repository-owned parser [codex-scripts/iso_splash_audit.py](../../codex-scripts/iso_splash_audit.py). It follows the public Syslinux `lss16toppm` layout and run decoding while enforcing a 16-million-pixel ceiling, rejecting truncated streams, and reporting run clipping/trailing payload. The historical `lss16toppm` program was not executed. The official Syslinux source distribution index is [kernel.org](https://www.kernel.org/pub/linux/utils/boot/syslinux/); the source implementation for `lss16toppm` is visible in the archived 6.03 source [here](https://fossies.org/linux/misc/old/syslinux-6.03.tar.gz/syslinux-6.03/utils/lss16toppm), and its purpose is documented in the [utility man page](https://manpages.debian.org/testing/syslinux-utils/lss16toppm.1.en.html).

Observed header: 56 bytes total; magic bytes `3d f3 13 14` (little-endian `0x1413f33d`), dimensions 640×300, then 16 RGB triplets in 6-bit component values (0–63). The RLE payload is 14,064 bytes. All 14,064 were consumed; there are zero complete trailing bytes, no unused nibble after the last pixel, no truncated input, and no overlong runs. Pixel indices used are 0–6 and 8–14; indices 7 and 15 do not occur. The parser emitted a 640×300 RGB PNG and binary P6 PPM; PPM payload is exactly 576,000 bytes.

Pixel comparison: decoded output matches [archives/neuroretransmit-cicada/2013/002-3301/splash.png](https://github.com/cicada-solvers/neuroretransmit-cicada/blob/b13600373d3400d75ec4208198019f3d92a304dd/2013/002-3301/splash.png) and `splash.ppm` exactly (all 640×300 RGB pixels identical). The extracted RLE is byte-identical to the nested archive’s `3301-contents/boot/isolinux/splash.rle`, SHA-256 `fe97dde87c511c67b5d793caa062704b9ab782559c9f0af3799b152f2a822614`. [archives/archive2/assets/2013/SPLASH.jpg](https://github.com/cicada-solvers/The-Complete-Cicada3301-Archive/blob/c3127e0e9eb5afbb582d37988d6369fe38429238/assets/2013/SPLASH.jpg) and [archives/archive2/EXTRA WIKI PAGES/DEBATE ABOUT MEANING OF THE PICTURE ON ONION/collage images origin/cicada/Copy_of_SPLASH.jpg](https://github.com/cicada-solvers/The-Complete-Cicada3301-Archive/blob/c3127e0e9eb5afbb582d37988d6369fe38429238/EXTRA%20WIKI%20PAGES/DEBATE%20ABOUT%20MEANING%20OF%20THE%20PICTURE%20ON%20ONION/collage%20images%20origin/cicada/Copy_of_SPLASH.jpg) are both 640×300 and agree visually; the decoded-vs-JPEG grayscale MAE is 0.1243, consistent with JPEG re-encoding. The other copy, [archives/archive2/EXTRA WIKI PAGES/Origin of Cicada 3301 logo/Images/Copy_of_SPLASH_copy.jpg](https://github.com/cicada-solvers/The-Complete-Cicada3301-Archive/blob/c3127e0e9eb5afbb582d37988d6369fe38429238/EXTRA%20WIKI%20PAGES/Origin%20of%20Cicada%203301%20logo/Images/Copy_of_SPLASH_copy.jpg), is a 640×600 composite stacking dark-background and light-background renditions.
